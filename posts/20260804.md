# Locus-local writers and the r0-up/r1-down learning loop


Dialogue, activation memory, and project authority are separate planes. Each
activation writes only at its locus repository; cross-repository movement is
pull-only. Home reads registered r0 upward and project activations read canonical
r1+ downward. Activation, peer, and cursor registries are distinct, and a reader
advances its cursor only after successful processing.


Operational endpoint trust (`registered-repo`) is not authorship proof. Optional
`signed-activation` is a stronger future trust mode.


The earlier proposal to place all continuity refs physically in the agent home
was superseded by the locus-local writer rule. Engine/surface-qualified ref
spellings remained transitional and were superseded on 5 August.
